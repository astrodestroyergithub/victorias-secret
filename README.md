## ✨ Key Features & Architecture

1. **Pixel-Accurate Victoria's Secret & PINK Aesthetic**
   - Iconic blush-pink and black ribbon stripe motifs (`vs-iconic-stripes`), luxury editorial typography (`Playfair Display` + `Inter`), and high-fashion studio imagery.
   - Brand switcher bar at the top for seamless filtering between **Victoria's Secret**, **PINK**, and **VS Beauty**.
   - Custom vector SVG monogram crest favicon (`assets/favicon.svg`) pre-wired for desktop browsers and iOS/Android home screens.

2. **Unbreakable Multi-Resolution Responsiveness**
   - **Mobile Phones (`320px – 767px`):** Dedicated bottom app navigation bar, slide-out hamburger department drawer, touch-friendly color/size selectors, and full-width search bar.
   - **Tablets (`768px – 1023px`):** 2-column adaptive product grid, slide-over shopping bag drawer, and responsive modal dialogs.
   - **Desktops & 4K Screens (`1024px – 2560px+`):** Full category mega-menu bar, 3-to-4 column product showcase, and split-screen editorial hero campaign.

3. **Complete E-Commerce & Interactive Salon Suite**
   - **Interactive Catalog:** 12 curated luxury items with live search, category pills, price range filter, bestseller toggle, and multi-criteria sorting.
   - **Per-Card Shade & Size Selection:** Choose color swatches and sizes (`XS–XXL`, `32B–38DD`, `50ml/100ml`) directly on product cards or inside the Quick View modal.
   - **Interactive Bra & Sleepwear Fit Calculator:** Computes exact US band/cup size and sleepwear size from underbust and bust measurements, with one-click catalog sync.
   - **Master Perfumer Scent Matcher:** Interactive quiz that recommends your signature fragrance based on olfactory family and mood.
   - **VS & PINK Collective Rewards:** Live points balance (`1,450 PTS`) with instant reward voucher redemption (`VSANGEL25`, `BOMBSHELL15`, `FREESHIP`).
   - **Multi-Currency Engine:** Instant price conversion across `USD ($)`, `INR (₹)`, `EUR (€)`, and `GBP (£)`.
   - **Shopping Bag & Encrypted Checkout Flow:** Free express shipping progress bar, signature gift box toggle, promo code validation, and instant order confirmation.

## 🚀 Deploying to Netlify

### Method 1: Netlify Drag & Drop (Fastest — 15 Seconds)
1. Unzip `victorias-secret-netlify-bundle.zip`.
2. Log in to [app.netlify.com](https://app.netlify.com/drop).
3. Drag and drop the unzipped folder directly onto the Netlify Drop zone.
4. Your site will go live immediately with custom headers and routing rules from `netlify.toml`.

### Method 2: Git Repository Deployment
1. Push the contents of this folder to a GitHub, GitLab, or Bitbucket repository.
2. In Netlify, click **Add new site → Import an existing project**.
3. Leave the build command blank (or keep the default in `netlify.toml`) and set the publish directory to `.`.
